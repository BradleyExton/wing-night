Round Cards (TV SETUP) — Prototype Round

Four directions for giving each of the eight round cards on the lobby screen a picture of its game, plus today's card (00) for comparison. Every page is the whole SETUP stage at 1920×1080 with the pack's real 2026-09-24 schedule, so the cards are judged against the wordmark, the flame and the floor the cast parades on, not in isolation.

The question these answer: today a card is three lines of type and a pill, and the only thing that tells SCHLONIC from GEO is the word in the pill. These four disagree about what the picture should be: a symbol (01), a painted moment of the game (02), a real frame of the game on the TV (03), or art-first cards in a new layout (04). 01 costs no height; 02 and 03 grow each card by roughly a quarter; 04 trades the 4×2 grid for one row of eight. All the art in 01, 02 and 04 is hand-drawn SVG in the cast's flat hand and lives in kit.js, so whichever wins can be tuned per game.

01

Neon Glyph

Same footprint, same words. The corner numeral becomes a small neon sign of the game's symbol (a drumstick with speed lines, a map pin, a speech bubble, a record, a pencil, a flapping bird, a framed picture, a slingshot) in the marquee's tube light, so the lobby already speaks the chrome the games wear. The signs hum, one buzzes. The quietest change and the cheapest.

Symbol, not picture: it tells the games apart but doesn't show what playing one looks like.
02

Diorama Window

Each card opens a window onto a painted moment of its game: the hen running the checkered shore with drumettes overhead, a photo and two pins on the dark map, three emoji tiles and blanks, a record and an equalizer, a doodle on paper, the flapping bird between two cliffs, a real and a fake in gilt frames, the slingshot and the tower. The sauce and round ride a smoked sill at the foot; the game is a chip on the art.

Eight small illustrations to own and keep in step with the games. Cards grow ~25% taller; the floor still clears at 1080p.
03

Game Still

No new art: each card is a real frame of its game on the TV, captured from the /dev/minigame sandbox and toned into the hearth's orange so eight different games still read as one lineup. The shipped text sits on it unchanged. Click or press C to see the frames in raw colour.

Captured from the committed sample pack (no party photos), zoomed past each game's marquee. Finding: SCHLONIC, DRAWING and EMOJI read; GEO, WHO'S THAT SONG and FORGERY STUDIO read as nothing, because their TVs are type and dark space designed for full-screen. Stills also go stale whenever a game's look changes.
04

Trading Card

The lineup as a hand of eight: one row of portrait cards, art first, read left to right like the night. Same painted scenes as 02, cropped square, with a numbered corner badge, the game as a banner across the art, the sauce as the name plate, a line in the show's voice ("Flap to the far cliff.") and a chilli heat rating from the round's points.

A new layout, not a new card: the 4×2 grid goes, and with it the "8 seats, 6 filled" placeholder reading, which would need redrawing as face-down cards.
