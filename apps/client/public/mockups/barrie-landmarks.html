Barrie landmarks — @wingnight/scenery contact sheet

Five new landmarks, each rendered here straight from its component with renderToStaticMarkup, so this page is the code, not a hand copy of it. Each is drawn twice: in SCHLONIC's summer morning (DESIGN.md §2.11, colours from SchlonicScene/palette.ts) and in JOUST's dusk silhouette (§2.7, colours from JoustArenaScene/palette.ts), at roughly TV density (11 px a world unit; a 1080p TV draws a 160-unit zone at 12). Neither scene stands these yet — the palette mappings here are a proposal in each game's existing colours. The last two frames stand them together in a 160 × 90 zone at half TV size, the condos at skyline scale.
