WARDROBE — metal · Molten Metal (teamD, chrome) · the spiky silhouette

The round. The portal's wardrobe (docs/guest-portal-team-pick-plan.md, decision 7) gives each guest two slots, torso and wing, with a rail per genre. This page draws metal's first-cut rail — battle vest, chain-mail shirt, spiked bracer, guitar pick patch — on the bird metal actually IS now: the spiky silhouette from Character/shapes, mitered join, three blade tail, two back spikes, pointed wing tips. The silhouette is the genre's statement; an item is the player's flavour on top, and it must sit with that shape rather than repeat it. Every item is a bodyApparel: painted after the body and the wing and before the neck and head, so it rides the body under a photographed jaw. Nothing on this page crosses a face, so crossesTheFace is never needed.

The rules are the September round's, listed in the first card and measured on every card from the item's drawn outline. Two things worth knowing before the 76px row: chrome (#d9dee6) is nearly white, so a white item has no fill contrast and lives on its 2.4 ink stroke; and metal's round 2 found that the bird has exactly one flat plane, the wing, and a second item has to claim a different anatomy. The torso slot and the wing slot are that split made literal.
