POP · Spice Girls — getting the team off the face

Today pop wears star shades: two white five-point stars on the eye line. The stars are fine. The placement is fatal — they cross the face, so apparelCrossesTheFace drops them on every player with a generated likeness, and on the actual night the pop team wears nothing at all. Colour is its only genre read, and colour alone is not a genre.

So the round is not "better sunglasses". Pop is stage production, not costume. Every candidate below lives from the shoulders down or off the bird entirely, so a drawn head and a photographed head wear exactly the same thing — and the star motif survives by moving, not by being replaced.
