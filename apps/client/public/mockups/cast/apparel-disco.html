APPAREL — disco · Disco Inferno (teamB)

Tonight disco wears a chain and a 4.6 disc on the chest. It is the only prop in the set that clears every rule: it hangs below the chin so it survives onto a costume head, it sits inside the silhouette at both anchors, and it degrades cleanly. It replaced a pair of floating white lapel wedges that hung off the bird's edge. So this page is not a repair job — it asks whether the safe prop is the right one. Two reasons to doubt it. First, a medallion is a necklace, and the note on metal's collar is "don't love the necklaces"; if metal moves off the neck, disco is left as the only bird in the room wearing jewellery. Second, look at the 76px row below: disco is the loudest genre in the world and currently owns the quietest prop in the set — about five pixels of white on a cyan chest, and no chain at all. The thesis here is that disco's prop should stop being worn on the chest and start being somewhere else on the bird — over its head, at its feet, or around its neckline as a shape big enough to cast a silhouette. Every candidate uses only white, the outline ink and the team colour; none needs a gradient, and the one that most wants glitter gets it from facet lines that are allowed to disappear.
