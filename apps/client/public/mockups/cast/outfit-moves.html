OUTFIT — Moves · the seventh slot

The round. Run 1b of the wardrobe build (docs/bird-wardrobe-build-plan.md): every critic fix applied; after critic round 1 the Horns pump became a Fist pump, the jabs of Point and swoon became out-and-back thrusts, the Cotton-Eye kick was replaced by a whole-body Bronco buck, and the Bump's tail was turned so it never crosses the partner's face. A Move is a signature flourish one bar long (2 s at 120 bpm) that fires in the lobby parade every ~8 bars and once at the intro lineup (docs/research/bird-wardrobe.md §2.1, §2.2). Each one is drawn on the parted rig (rig.html): six parts — tail, far leg, body, near leg, wing, head-with-neck — each turned and nudged about its own fixed pivot. There is no whole-bird layer here at all: a lean is every upper part turned by the same angle about the hips (each part's own translate + rotate, computed), a hop is every part lifted. That is what a cast pose can carry, so every Move ports to CharacterFigure as a keyframe set per part.

Reading a card. The live loop runs the bar over and over at 130px (the lobby parade) and 173px (the intro lineup); the four cells under it are the four beats. Below it, six key frames at 130px, in colour on the stage and then in pure silhouette (the test: the Move has to read as a shape). Every Move starts and ends at rest so it splices into whatever dance the bird is doing. The rules line is measured off the rendered geometry every 20 ms through the bar: the photo's total turn (head plus lean) stays within ±40°, and nothing, stroke included, drops below the foot line at y 72.75. Add ?move=<id> for one Move, ?head=rob|sara|kenny for another face, ?t=0.5 to park the live loops on one moment of the bar.
