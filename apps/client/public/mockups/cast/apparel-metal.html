APPAREL — metal · Molten Metal (teamD, chrome) · round 2

Round 1 lost. It replaced the studded collar — a dark crescent under the chin that read as a necklace — with THE CUT, a battle vest, and the vest was rejected: "the vest doesn't really fit well with the chickens." The round 1 cards are still on this page, below, as the record. This is what replaces them.

Why it lost, and the new rule. The vest out-weighed the bird. A team is its colour, and at 76px a metal bird became a dark mass with a bright square on it while chrome — the team's actual colour — nearly disappeared. So round 2 has a hard constraint the first round did not: the team colour must stay the dominant area of the bird. That is not left to taste here: the page rasterises every candidate at TV size, counts the pixels that are still the team's colour, and prints the result against the SAME bird wearing nothing — so the outline, the legs and the belly shade, which are not colour either and are identical in every row, cancel out. A bare bird is …; tonight's collar leaves … of the colour standing; round 1's rejected vest leaves …. Anything down at the vest's number is out.

Two other things went wrong and both are about anatomy. A cut rectangle fights a round bird — hard edges do make a dark shape read as cloth, but laid across a creature made entirely of curves they read as a saddle. And the vest sat exactly where the wing is: same colour, same place, so the two merged and the bird lost a limb. Round 2 works with the anatomy instead — the wing's own plane, the wing's own axis, the bird's own outline — and keeps every prop small enough that the colour still wins.
