WARDROBE — disco · Disco Inferno (teamB) · the preener silhouette

The round. The portal's wardrobe (docs/guest-portal-team-pick-plan.md, decision 7) gives each guest two slots, torso and wing, with a rail per genre. This page draws disco's first-cut rail — open shirt with one wedge, sequin shirt, medallion on the wing, tambourine — on the bird disco actually is now: the preener silhouette from Character/shapes, the tall leggy one, chest up high, two sickle tail feathers swept up, and a wing that is a thin crescent rather than a plate. That wing is the constraint on this page: it is eight or nine units across at its widest, so a wing item here is a 9-unit mark or it hangs off the wing onto the body. Every item is a bodyApparel: painted after the body and the wing and before the neck and head, so it rides the body under a photographed jaw. Nothing here crosses a face.

The rules are the September round's, listed in the first card and measured on every card from the item's drawn outline. Disco's own round settled two things this page inherits: one white wedge on the chest, never two (the butterfly collar read as fangs on sight), and the medallion on the chest is a necklace — so here the medallion is moved to the wing, where the question becomes whether a white disc on a wing is a coin or an eye.
