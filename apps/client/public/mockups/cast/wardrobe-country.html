The round. The portal's wardrobe (docs/guest-portal-team-pick-plan.md, decision 7) gives each guest two slots, torso and wing, with a rail per genre. This page draws country's first-cut rail — plaid shirt, bandana down the chest, horseshoe patch, banjo — on the bird country actually is now: the broody silhouette from Character/shapes, the squat one, belly down near the toes, a small tucked wing and two low tail plumes. That body changes the maths for the rail: the chest is wider than the round bird's (its edge is at x≈69 at the waist) and the wing is the smallest of the four, a 22-unit tuck, so a wing item has half the plane pop's star had. Every item is a bodyApparel: painted after the body and the wing and before the neck and head, so it rides the body under a photographed jaw. Nothing here crosses a face; country's own round already buried the hat for that reason.

The rules are the September round's, listed in the first card and measured on every card from the item's drawn outline. The two about white matter most here, because country's good ideas are mostly white shapes with something inside them: a horseshoe is a ring and a banjo head is a disc, and both are exactly the "white with a dark centre" an eye is. They are drawn anyway and the note on each says where it fails.
