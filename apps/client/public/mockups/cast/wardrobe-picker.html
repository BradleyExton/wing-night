WARDROBE PICKER — seven slots, a rail per team

The recommendation in docs/research/bird-wardrobe.md, drawn. Left: /me/wardrobe on a phone. Your bird stands in its stage at the top (tap it to play its Move, tap TV size to see it as the room will). Under it are the team's Looks and a Dress me dice, then seven slot tabs and a grid where every tile is a zoomed crop of your own bird wearing that item. Tapping a tile equips it, with no Save button. At the foot, your teammates dance in what they're wearing. Right: the same bird at 130px (the lobby parade), blurred 130px (about 3 m from a 55" set) and 76px (the cut line), with every Look on the team at 76px.

The items are quick drawings that show where each slot lives: the kit (Top, Sleeve, Tail, Boots) that rides the bird everywhere, and the showpieces (Prop, Stage, Move) that only appear on show. They are not the round's art; milestone 2b draws and judges them. Switching team swaps the rail and keeps each team's outfit, which is the plan's decision 6.
