Wardrobe · Gate 1 cut sheet

What this is. Every item of the seven-slot bird wardrobe that run 1 drew (docs/bird-wardrobe-build-plan.md, docs/research/bird-wardrobe.md §2), one tile per variant, with the critics' last verdict on it. You keep or cut each one; Copy my cuts at the foot copies the whole list as plain text for you to paste back into the chat. Your toggles are saved in this browser.

How to judge. Each tile shows the default style at 76px with the drawn head, then 76px with a costume (photo) head, then 130px blurred. 76px is the bird's actual size on a 1080p TV, and it is the cut line: if it doesn't read at 76, it doesn't ship. The blurred 130 is the parade seen from about 3 m away on a 55" TV. Any other styles follow at 76px on the photo head. The coloured edge and badge are the critic's verdict (keep, fix, cut); the toggle starts on keep only for a critic keep. A fix means the critic asked for a change: the tile shows the art as it is on disk now, and "why" holds the change it asked for. The measured line is the slot rules (chin, hair, floor, region, prop sweep), measured on the drawn geometry.

The open gold question. Gold (#d4a72c) is not a house token. A few items offer it as an optional style, never the default; those tiles are marked gold · open question and show the gold style. Critics so far: on metal it looks like ember, and on country it is 1.30:1 against the orange.

Below the items: the Looks (every slot of a kit worn at once, the density test), then the Moves, looping live at 130px from outfit-moves.html. The full cards with the 304px rows live on the genre pages (metal, pop, country, disco).
