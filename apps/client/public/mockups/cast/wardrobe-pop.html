WARDROBE — pop · Spice Girls (teamH, pink) · the stock bird

The round. The portal's wardrobe (docs/guest-portal-team-pick-plan.md, decision 7) gives each guest two slots, torso and wing, with a rail per genre. This page draws pop's first-cut rail — crop jacket, sequin tee, star patch, mic — on the bird pop actually is: resolveTeamSilhouette gives pop no silhouette, because "smooth, round, upright" is a description of the stock bird, and pop carries its genre in motion (the team bounce) instead. So pop's items have a job the other three rails do not: with no silhouette doing the talking, the item is the only thing on the bird that says pop. Every item is a bodyApparel: painted after the body and the wing and before the neck and head, so it rides the body under a photographed jaw. Nothing here crosses a face.

The rules are the September round's, listed in the first card and measured on every card from the item's drawn outline. Pop's own round found two things that still hold: the wing is the one flat plane and a 12-unit star is the biggest single mark it takes; and white props die on chrome, which pop is not, but the rail should not assume a colour — pink is the pack's, not the genre's.
