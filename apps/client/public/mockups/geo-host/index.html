Geo Host (tablet) — Mockups

Two takes on the host takeover for GEO, both in the host shell's own language rather than the field-journal page. Click anywhere to toggle the submitted state.

The question these answer: the host page's layout already works — landscape, never scrolls, one action per turn. What is wrong with it is the costume: serif type, a gold double-rule border, a white rotated polaroid, sepia tiles and a rotated "off course" stamp. Both variants keep the working shape and swap the costume for the §2.0A shell language TRIVIA already uses — mini-rail chips, rounded house cards, a single primary CTA — and disagree about how much tablet the map should get. View at 1024×768. Both keep the bottom-right corner-dock gutter clear.

01

Deck

Today's two columns, re-skinned. Mini-rail chips on top, then a photo card + caption + hint + orange CTA on the left, map on the right; the map column becomes stat tiles over a small reveal map once the pin is stamped. Smallest diff — structurally the surface that ships today, just dressed as the rest of the app.

02

Map First

The map is the whole tablet and everything else floats on it as glass: rail top-left, quick views top-right, photo as a card that can shrink once the team has looked at it, and the CTA as a pill bottom-left. Roughly doubles the map area a thumb has to work with, at the cost of chrome sitting over the thing being tapped — and on reveal the photo card can land on top of a pin, as it does in this mockup.
