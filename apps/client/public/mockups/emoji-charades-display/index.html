Emoji Charades Display (TV) — Mockups

Three takes on the TV surface for the EMOJI_CHARADES minigame. All show the same 20-emoji clue mid-turn, with the marquee HUD, per-team standings footer, and the post-result reveal overlay. Click anywhere to toggle the reveal.

The question these answer: how does a growing emoji sequence read from across the room, and what happens as it approaches the 30-emoji cap? Each variant answers differently — shrink, wrap, or drop. View full-screen; typography is clamp-scaled against a 4K baseline per DESIGN.md §4.1. The display never receives the subject text outside the 2s reveal window.

01

Hero Line

One giant centred line that shrinks to fit as the clue grows. Maximum drama at three emoji; this mockup shows twenty so the cost is visible rather than assumed. Simplest to build and the most fragile at the cap.

02

Clue Wall

Constant emoji size, wrapped into a fixed 6×5 board of all thirty slots — filled ones lit, the remainder ghosted, newest ringed in gold. Nothing ever shrinks and the room can see how much clue is left, at the price of a quieter reveal moment and a board that reads busy when it's nearly full.

03

Ribbon

Recency-weighted. The newest emoji lands huge on the right with a pop, older ones shrink and fade away to the left, and anything past the last nine collapses into an "N earlier" marker. Deliberately refuses to show all thirty equally — bets that in charades the room only needs the latest addition big.
