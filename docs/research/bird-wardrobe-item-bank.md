# Bird wardrobe: the item idea bank

Date: 2026-10-14. Appendix to [bird-wardrobe.md](bird-wardrobe.md), which picks the v1 rails from
this bank. Every row is an idea, not a commitment: anything that reaches a rail is still drawn in the
harness and judged on the 76px row first. "Slot" uses the research pass's own tags (torso, wing-worn,
wing-held, tail, legs-feet, back, ground-stage, aura-particle, motion-emote), which map onto the
recommended slots as torso → Top, wing-worn → Sleeve, wing-held → Prop, tail → Tail, legs-feet →
Boots, ground-stage → Stage, motion-emote → Move; back and aura-particle are left out of v1
(bird-wardrobe.md §2.9). The "76px" column is the research pass's guess, before any drawing.


The current first-cut rail from the plan is marked **[rail]**, and harness survivors **[survived]**.

## 7. The research pass's own recommendations (superseded by bird-wardrobe.md §2)

1. **Add one instrument or prop per rail first**, since it is the strongest secondary read: Flying V
   (metal), star mic or drumstick mic (pop), banjo or lasso (country), mirror ball or tambourine
   (disco).
2. **The best torso candidates not yet on a rail:** metal bullet-belt bandolier (M3), pop Union
   Jack dress (P1) and tracksuit stripes (P3), country fringe on the wing (C5) and trophy buckle
   (C7), disco butterfly collar (D3) and chest medallion (D6). Each is either an outline breaker
   or one big flat shape.
3. **Probably cut on the 76px row:** chain mail (M8), plaid (C2 as real plaid), leopard with
   realistic spots (P2), bolo tie (C8), anything that depends on rhinestone or stud detail.
4. **New layers to decide on before drawing more:** a ground element (the lit disco floor and the
   hay bale are both strong), a tail-accessory slot, an aura/particle layer, and a per-guest
   flourish. These are product decisions for Brad, not harness items. The plan currently allows
   only `torso` and `wing`.
5. **One exception test for feet:** platform trainers/shoes, shared by pop and disco. They're the
   Spice Girls' shared motif, and disco's too, which is why they're the only feet item that
   justifies breaking rule 3. They can't move `CHARACTER_FOOT`.
6. **Watch the cross-team collisions:** star (pop vs country sheriff star), flame (metal pyro vs
   disco inferno vs the heat aura), glitter (pop confetti vs disco sparkle), neon (disco vs a
   honky-tonk sign), leopard (pop Scary vs glam metal), guitars (Flying V vs acoustic), feather boa
   (pop vs disco). Give each motif one owner, or a different material per team.
7. **Team-paint every item** (the TF2 rule), so a disco bird in a borrowed shared item never reads
   as metal.
